---
name: NativeTabs opaque appearance
description: How to drive expo-router's iOS 26 Liquid Glass tab bar from app state, why backgroundColor alone is not enough, how to verify without a device, and what has not been confirmed on one.
---

**Rule:** To solidify expo-router's iOS 26 `NativeTabs` bar from an in-app preference, pass `backgroundColor`, `blurEffect="none"` and `disableTransparentOnScrollEdge` together (plus `shadowColor` for the top hairline). Pass none of them to keep the default Liquid Glass. When recoloring items, pass `tintColor` together with the object forms of `iconColor` / `labelStyle` (`{ default, selected }`) so both item states are pinned rather than relying on expo-router's selected-falls-back-to-tintColor rule.

**Why:** expo-router builds two `UITabBarAppearance` objects per tab. The scroll-edge one drops `backgroundColor`, forces `blurEffect` to `none` and makes the shadow transparent unless `disableTransparentOnScrollEdge` is set, so `backgroundColor` alone leaves the bar clear whenever content sits at the scroll edge (most of the time for short screens). `blurEffect="none"` becomes `backgroundEffect = nil`; a custom background plus nil effect is UIKit's documented way to opt a bar out of Liquid Glass (WWDC25 "Build a UIKit app with the new design"). Native color conversion keeps rgba alpha, so a translucent panel token works as the background. iOS's own system Reduce Transparency solidifies the glass by itself and never sees in-app preferences, which is why the layout has to ask. Owner rule: high contrast gets the translucent panel, never an opaque bar; only Reduce transparency makes surfaces opaque.

**Verify without a device:** expo-router's iOS appearance builders import cleanly under jest-expo, so a suite can feed the layout's props through the real ones and assert the derived per-state colors and bar background in both appearances. Do not import expo-router's native-tabs `utils` for the prop converters: it pulls the vendored react-navigation core (and its `query-string` ESM problem); mirror the small conversion instead, typed against the builders' own parameter type (`NativeTabOptions` is not exported from the public entry point).

**Not device-confirmed:** no iOS 26 device or simulator is reachable from this workspace (probed 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-20; do not re-probe, file a BLOCKED record). Open questions only a phone answers: whether UIKit draws the opted-out bar full-width or as a filled capsule, and whether unselected icons take `iconColor.default` once the bar has left the glass — react-native-screens documents that on the glass bar iOS 26 applies the item icon color to the selected item only (the title color override is unconditional).

**How to apply:** any further NativeTabs appearance work goes through the same props and the same two suites (mocked-navigator props per toggle state; real-builder derivation). Keep the device-check records in the Chat App `docs/` folder current when a phone becomes available.
